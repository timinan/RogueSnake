Each run starts by picking a head ability — LASER hit-scan, ICE spreading cone, or FLAME homing fireball. Then the snake spawns with all three body specials (Magnet, Shield, Echo). SPACE fires the head; ECHO segments behind it amplify the shot. Each enemy's white health ring loses a segment per hit.
