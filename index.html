Pick a head ability at the start — LASER, ICE, or FLAME. The snake starts with the head only — one hit and you die, so dodge. Each stage is waves of enemies; clear them all and you earn a body special. New stages unlock new enemy kinds, ramp wave size, and zoom the camera out — by stage 7+ much bigger, tougher heavies appear.
