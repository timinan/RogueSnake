Pick a head — LASER, ICE, or FLAME. SHIFT dashes through danger. Each body segment also acts as a modifier for the segment in front of it:
M behind a shot = aim at the nearest enemy.
S behind any fire = bonus shockwave at the source.
E behind any fire = re-fire from the echo's position.
» behind M/S = cooldown 40% faster.
Order matters: [HEAD][M][E] ≠ [HEAD][E][M].
